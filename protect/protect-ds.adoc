=  Protect a VMFS Datastore
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Protect eligible VMFS datastores by adding them to a protection group, enabling SnapMirror replication and failover capabilities.

.Before you begin

Ensure the datastore uses the iSCSI or FC protocol.
Confirm you have administrative privileges in vSphere.
Verify that the datastore is not already part of a protection group.

.Steps

. . In the vSphere client, right-click the VMFS datastore you want to protect and select Protect.
. The system checks the datastore’s eligibility for protection.

If the datastore is ineligible, a message displays the reasons and you cannot proceed.
. Choose to add the datastore to an existing protection group or create a new protection group.
. If creating a new protection group:
.. Enter a name for the group.
.. Configure SnapMirror relationship settings as required.
. Click Protect to start the protection workflow.
[NOTE]
The Protect option is only available for eligible VMFS datastores using iSCSI or FC protocols.

.Result
The datastore is protected and appears in the selected protection group. You can view or manage its protection status from the ONTAP tools plugin.
