=  Unprotect a VMFS datastore
:icons: font
:imagesdir: ../media/

[.lead]
Remove a datastore from a protection group, with options to delete associated target artifacts and protection settings.

.Before you begin

Ensure you have administrative privileges in vSphere.
Review the impact of unprotecting the last datastore in a group.

.Steps

. In the vSphere client, right-click the protected datastore and select Unprotect.
. Confirm whether to delete replicated LUNs and consistency groups on the target.
. Click Unprotect to proceed.
. If unprotecting the last datastore in a group, acknowledge the warning that the protection group will be deleted.

[NOTE]
Unprotecting the last datastore deletes the protection group and associated target artifacts.
.Result
The datastore is removed from protection. If it was the last in the group, the group and target artifacts are deleted.
